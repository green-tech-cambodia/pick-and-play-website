Frequently asked questions

How do I start a lesson?

Open the app, choose an instrument (Guitar, Piano, Drum or Violin), then pick a lesson or a free class and tap play.

Are the free classes really free?

Yes. Classes marked Free Class can be watched without payment.

How do I buy an instrument or accessory?

Go to Products, choose an item, and follow the order steps in the app. For questions about an order, contact us with your order details.

How does bidding work?

Open Bidding, choose an item and place your bid before the auction ends. The highest bid when the auction closes wins the item.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. You can also email us and we will delete your account and personal data.

Which languages does the app support?

The app is available in Khmer and English.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

1. Who we are

This policy explains how the Pick & Play mobile app (“Pick & Play”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your information.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, phone number and profile photo when you create an account.
  • Order and bidding information: items you buy or bid on, delivery address and contact details needed to complete an order.
  • Learning activity: courses you join, lessons watched and practice progress.
  • Device and usage information: device type, app version and basic diagnostic data to fix errors and improve the app.
  • Microphone (optional): used only during chord practice to check the sound you play. Audio is processed for this feature and is not stored or shared.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide lessons, classes, chord practice and your learning progress.
  • To process orders and bids, and to contact you about them.
  • To send notifications you have agreed to receive.
  • To answer support requests and improve the app.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the app (such as hosting, payment and delivery partners), and only as needed to provide the service, or when required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Settings → Delete Account, or by contacting us. We then delete your personal data, except what we must keep by law (for example, order records).

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect your information.

7. Children

Pick & Play is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email greentechnology.cambodia@gmail.com.
